# Macro Mispricing Radar

**Live page:** https://eshwarpotturi.github.io/macro-mispricing-radar/

Step 1 (Economy) of a top-down Economy → Industry → Company screen for asset managers. Step 2 and 3: [Broker Track Record](https://eshwarpotturi.github.io/broker-track-record/). Companion to the [Fed Claim Catalog](https://eshwarpotturi.github.io/fed-claim-catalog/).

Forecasters tell markets how each economy will do, and markets price that in. When an economy then delivers less but its stock market does not come down, the market has not caught up. The page scores 29 equity markets on that idea, lets the reader pick the signals and horizon (3–5 years or next year), and shows how the rule has done every year since 2012.

**Forecasters:** IMF World Economic Outlook (every release 2008–2026), European Commission AMECO archive (28 releases 2011–2025, 16 markets), OECD Economic Outlook (Nov 2023 onward), World Bank GEP (2024, 2026).

**Finding added in this version:** on the same 128 one-year-ahead GDP calls (16 markets, 2013–2024), the IMF and the European Commission are about equally accurate (average miss 0.64 vs 0.69 pts). The OECD's lead in 2024–25 rests on two years only.

## How it works

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `pipeline/fetch_macro.py` | Downloads IMF and OECD vintages (via DBnomics), the AMECO forecast archive and World Bank series into `data/raw/` (not committed). Needs `pip install truststore` on a corporate network. |
| `pipeline/tidy.py` | One row per forecast → `data/forecasts.csv` |
| `pipeline/errors.py` | Each forecast minus the first published actual → `data/errors.csv` (2020–21 excluded) |
| `pipeline/harden.py` | Adds the EC to the consensus, its correction, the next-year signal and the forecaster comparison. Reads `data/page_data_base.json` (the original page's data), writes `data/page_data.json` |
| `build.py` | `site/template.html` + `data/page_data.json` → `index.html` |

To update: `python pipeline/fetch_macro.py && python pipeline/tidy.py && python pipeline/errors.py && python pipeline/harden.py && python build.py`

## Limits

- Market signals (growth vs forecast, stocks vs economy, market cap / GDP) and the backtest come from the original build and are not yet regenerated by this pipeline.
- The OECD, World Bank and EC do not forecast Singapore or Hong Kong; those rely on the IMF. Asian Development Bank forecasts were blocked on the office network.
- Three AMECO archive files (autumn 2015, autumn 2020, winter 2016) downloaded damaged and are skipped.

Not investment advice.
